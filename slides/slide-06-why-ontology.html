Background · Motivation

Why an Ontology for Planning?

An ontology is a formal, shared representation of concepts and their relationships. Modeling planning domains and planner capabilities this way turns scattered metadata into queryable, reusable knowledge.

🗂️

Organize Metadata

Capture domains, planners, heuristics & results in one structured model.

🔎

Reason & Query

Answer semantic questions over planning knowledge with SPARQL.

🚀

Improve Performance

Select promising planners; extract macros as action-ordering constraints.

💬

Explain & Share

Generate human-readable explanations and promote reuse across the community.
